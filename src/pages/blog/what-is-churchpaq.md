[**Churchpaq**](https://churchpaq.com) is a platform I built to help churches manage their daily operations in one place.

Many churches still handle administration with scattered tools: notebooks, spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups, and manual processes. That usually creates duplicate data, communication gaps, and extra work for volunteers and staff.

Churchpaq solves that by centralizing key workflows in a single system designed specifically for church teams. Instead of forcing churches to adapt to generic business software, the platform is shaped around how church communities actually operate.

It is also intentionally built to be easy to use and easy to understand, even for teams with limited technical experience.

The goal is simple: reduce administrative friction so leaders can spend less time on repetitive management tasks and more time serving people.

At a high level, [**Churchpaq**](https://churchpaq.com) helps churches:

- Organize records and member information
- Keep teams aligned with clearer operational processes
- Improve visibility into day-to-day activities
- Run administration with less manual overhead

If you are part of a church team and want to modernize your operations, you can learn more at [churchpaq.com](https://churchpaq.com).
